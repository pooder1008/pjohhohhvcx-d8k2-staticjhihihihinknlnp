What's New in V2.9

Every change from V2.8 to V2.9 -- plus how to use the new features.

New Features

NEW

8 Build Slots

Expanded from 6 to 8 slots. Save more player builds and switch between them mid-game with L1+R1+D-Pad.

How to use: Open the menu (L2+Options), navigate to the Build page, and select a slot 1-8. Quick switch during gameplay with L1+R1+D-Pad Up/Down/Left/Right.
NEW

Hesi Flick Dribble

New 7th dribble move that forces a speed boost off the hesitation. Available as the 5th option in Custom dribble mode.

How to use: Open the menu, go to Dribble Mode, set to Custom. Assign Hesi Flick to any D-Pad direction. Hold that direction during gameplay to trigger.
NEW

Adaptive Tempo

The script learns your shot rhythm during live gameplay. Each shot blends into your tempo using a weighted average (80% existing + 20% new shot). Standstill and fade timings tracked separately.

How to use: Open the menu, go to Adaptive Tempo page, toggle ON. Calibrate with KILLSHOT first to set your baseline, then Adaptive Tempo fine-tunes as you play.
NEW

Defense AI+ Enhanced

Now includes lateral slide boost when holding L2 for faster defensive movement, plus RS amplification on L2+R2 for better contests and steals.

How to use: Enable Defense AI+ in the menu. Hold L2 on defense for lateral slide boost. Hold L2+R2 together for RS amplification on contests.
