Loaded with Features

Rebuilt for how 2K27 actually scores a shot.

🎯

KILLSHOT Calibration

Stop guessing at numbers. Take a shot at your own release point, press Cross or A to confirm it, and KILLSHOT builds a five-rep average while dropping the highest and lowest values. Calibrate Standstill, Fade and No-Dip separately in your chosen shot method.

🎮

Rhythm Shooting Engine

You press the shot button, the script performs the Pro Stick motion for you: down, then a controlled travel up into the release. Identical tempo on every attempt, which is exactly what 2K27 rewards with a wider green window.

🎯

True Button Shooting

A full button method, not a stick trick wearing a button's clothes. The script holds your shot button for a calibrated duration and releases it at your green point, and never touches the Pro Stick. Separate Hold MS values cover Standstill, Fade and No-Dip.

📈

Timing and Tempo, Split

2K27 scores your release and your tempo separately and tells you which one you missed. So we store them separately: Timing sets when the shot goes, Tempo sets how fast the stick travels into it. Fix one without breaking the other.

⚡

Turbo Shots

Turbo Fade shots with quick-release RS flicks. Create space and score from anywhere on the court with optimized fade timing.

🏀

7 Dribble Moves + Chains

BTB Spam, Curry Slide, D Book Hesi, Ball Break, Escape, Momentum, and Hesi Flick. Hold D-Pad to auto-repeat with Combo Chains. Assign any move to any direction.

🛡

Defense AI+

Lateral slide boost on L2 for faster defensive slides. 1.3x Right Stick amplification on L2+R2 for better contests and steals.

🔄

Burst Stamina

Burst or Gradual stamina management. Full speed bursts followed by smart speed capping. Conserve energy without sacrificing explosiveness.

📺

Scrolling OLED Menu

Seven categories, 35 settings, nothing more than three steps away. Scroll a list of four rows at a time with a cursor and arrows instead of clicking through one screen per setting. The menu hides whatever does not apply to your shot method and skips straight past it, so you never scroll through settings that do nothing for you. Shot feedback on the OLED after every attempt.

🎯

Three Shot Contexts

Standstill, Fade on R2 and No-Dip on L2. A fade does not release when a standstill does, so each context stores its own calibrated value on both shot methods, per build slot. No-Dip Mode enables the No-Dip context.

🎯

Auto Green Shot Method

Choose AUTO GREEN as your Shot Method to use the rhythm engine with timed auto-release. Auto Green Timing controls when the shot releases, while Auto Green Tempo controls the stick travel into that release.

🔢

Absolute, Never Random

No stability values, no jitter, no randomization anywhere in the script. The same setting produces the same input on every single attempt. With 2K27's windows tighter than last year, deliberate variance can only push you out of the green.

✨

Per-Build Shot Method

Shot method is stored per build slot, so you can run Button on your big and Rhythm on your guard and switch between them mid-game with L1+R1 and the D-Pad. Nothing to re-toggle, nothing to re-enter.

🛡

Shot Cancel Intelligence

Filters pump fakes and accidental taps so the tempo system never fires on short presses. Also blocks rapid re-presses that cause the "go to shot" animation glitch.

🔒

Activation Lock

Discord-bound activation code system. Each buyer gets a unique 6-button code tied to their Discord ID. Anti-resale protection built in.
